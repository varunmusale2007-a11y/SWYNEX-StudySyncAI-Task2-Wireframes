StudySync AI – Task 2
Information Architecture & Low-Fidelity Wireframes
Project Overview

StudySync AI is an AI-powered learning platform designed to help students organize their studies, manage academic tasks, access learning resources, and receive AI-assisted study support.

This repository contains the UX work completed for Task 2: Information Architecture & Low-Fidelity Wireframes, including the information architecture, major screen wireframes, intended user flow, and key UX design decisions.

Task Objective

The objective of this task is to create the information architecture and low-fidelity wireframes for the major screens of StudySync AI and explain the intended user flow and important design decisions.

5. Important Design Decisions
1. Dashboard-First Experience

The dashboard provides a quick overview of the student's current tasks, schedule, and progress so that important information is available immediately after login.

2. Simple Navigation

The navigation structure is kept simple so students can quickly move between Dashboard, Study Planner, Subjects, Study AI, Progress, and Settings.

3. AI Assistance as a Core Feature

Study AI has a dedicated screen because AI assistance is a key part of the StudySync AI experience. Students can use it to understand topics, ask questions, and summarize study content.

4. Study Planning and Scheduling

The Study Planner and Calendar help students organize study sessions, deadlines, tasks, and other academic activities in a structured way.

5. Progress Tracking

The Progress screen provides students with an overview of their completed activities and overall learning progress.

6. Low-Fidelity Approach

The wireframes intentionally use basic shapes, placeholders, and grayscale elements. This keeps the focus on functionality, layout, navigation, and user flow before visual styling is introduced.

6. UX Goals

The main UX goals for StudySync AI are:

Make studying more organized
Reduce the effort required to manage study tasks
Provide quick access to AI assistance
Help students create structured study plans
Make academic progress easy to understand
Keep navigation simple and intuitive
